# Animation Library
## Idle
4–6 s seamless loop: breathing, tiny head drift, random blink.
## Listening
Subtle head inclination and attentive focus.
## Thinking
Brief eye shift and pause, 1.2–2 s.
## Speaking
Small head and shoulder accents; mouth driven independently.
## Welcome
Open both hands slightly and return.
## Present Wine
Use the hand nearest the wine card.
## Present Aroma
Open palm toward aroma constellation.
## Present Food
Open palm toward pairing cards.
## Goodbye
Warm smile, small nod, return to neutral.

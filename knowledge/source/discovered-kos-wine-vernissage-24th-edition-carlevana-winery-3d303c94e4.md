Wine Vernissage – 24th edition

On December 12, at 5 p.m., at the Palace of the Republic, the Wine Vernissage – 24th edition will take place.

Carlevana Winery will be present at the vernissage with select wines, to offer you an experience full of special aromas and flavors.

!!! For the first time, we will also present our new product – Viviano Brut Sparkling Wine.

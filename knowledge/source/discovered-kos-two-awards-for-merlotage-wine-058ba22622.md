Two awards for Merlotage wine

We would like to share some exciting news with you: our Raritet Merlotage wine has won two prestigious awards.

Raritet Merlotage 2022 received a gold medal at the Concours Mondiale Bruxelles 2025 competition and bronze medal at the Decanter World Wine Awards 2025.

We are delighted that Moldovan wine has been recognized at the international level.

Merlotage is a blend of three noble French varieties, based on Merlot, followed by Malbec and Cabernet Sauvignon. An exceptional wine, aged for 18 months. It is one of two red blends produced in Carlevana for over 20 years.

You can purchase this or other wines in our wine shop. We also invite you to a wine tour with degustation at the Carlevana Winery.

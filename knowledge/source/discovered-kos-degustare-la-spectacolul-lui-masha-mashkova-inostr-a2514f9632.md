Wine degustation at Masha Mashkova’s performance – Foreigner

Carlevana Winery invites you to enjoy a special evening of theater and wine.

On November 29, at the “Fără Nume” Theater in Chișinău, the play “Inostranka” will be performed by the talented Mashkova.

Before the show, we look forward to seeing you in the foyer for a Carlevana wine tasting—aroma, elegance, and emotion in a single glass.

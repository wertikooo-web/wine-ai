Fetească Regală grapes and wine

What is Fetească Regală?

Fetească Regală, translated from Romanian as “royal maiden” or “princess,” is a white wine grape variety belonging to the Vitis vinifera species. It was first cultivated in the 1920s in the village of Danes (near Sighișoara, Transylvania, Romania).

History of origin

The variety arose naturally as a result of crossing the Fetească Albă and Grasă de Cotnari varieties. It was previously believed that the second parent was Francușa, but recent research confirms the combination with Grasă.

According to legend, the variety was named “royal” after King Ferdinand I tasted the wine at an exhibition in Bucharest and suggested naming it Fetească Regală.

Regions where Fetească Regală is grown

Fetească Regală is one of the most common varieties in Romania, occupying about 12,000–13,000 hectares. It is actively cultivated in the following regions:

Transylvania

Republic of Moldova

Crișana

Banat

Muntenia

Outside Romania, the variety is also grown in Moldova, Slovakia, Hungary, Bulgaria, Austria, and even in certain regions of Ukraine.

Agronomic characteristics

The bush is medium-sized, with cylindrical-conical clusters.

The berries are yellow-green, medium in size.

The flowers are hermaphroditic, and the variety is self-fertile.

Moderately resistant to frost.

Susceptible to mildew and gray rot, requires control of humidity and ventilation in the vineyard.

Thanks to its plasticity and adaptability to different terroirs, Fetească Regală is well suited for both industrial and craft winemaking.

Wine from Fetească Regală

Aroma and taste

Fetească Regală produces wines with a refreshing, aromatic character. Depending on the technology used, the main notes can be identified as follows:

green apple

pear

citrus

minerality

floral notes

when aged in oak — almond, apricot, dried fruit

The alcohol content of the wine is usually 10–12.5%. The wine is light, balanced, with pleasant acidity and medium body.

Fetească Regală wine styles

Dry wine — fermented in steel, characterized by freshness and fruitiness.

Aged wine — fermented or aged in oak barrels, acquires roundness and complexity.

Sparkling wine — this variety is also suitable for the production of light, aromatic sparkling wines.

Food pairings

Fetească Regală wine goes well with:

seafood (oysters, mussels, squid)

fish dishes (salmon, sea bream, pike perch)

chicken and turkey dishes

soft cheeses (Brie, Camembert)

traditional Eastern European dishes (stuffed cabbage rolls, mushroom risotto, vegetable casseroles)

More mature wines are suitable for richer dishes, such as duck with sauce or baked eggplant.

Conclusion

Fetească Regală is a modern yet authentic variety that has become a symbol of Romanian and Moldovan white winemaking. It combines lightness, freshness, and aging potential. Thanks to its ability to adapt to different terroirs and technologies, this variety is gaining recognition not only among local winemakers but also on the international stage.

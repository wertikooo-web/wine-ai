A Terroir of Culture,
Creativity and Excellence

Castel Mimi

We are proud to share every day the joy of being the new generation among the best wine producers.

We are happy to keep open the gate of the most beautiful wine tourist destination in Moldova. The history, cultural values, drive for creativity and excellence in wine and tourism has placed Castel Mimi in the top 15 most beautiful wineries in the world.

Every year, more than 30,000 people from 80 countries around the world take home a souvenir of unforgettable events.

About white, red and rose, about traditional Moldovan cuisine combined with modern preparation techniques, about the legends of Mimi Castle - a world of wine, culture and excellence.

We invite you into the world of Castel Mimi, where your memory awaits you.

Collections

The Governor Series

We honor the memory of the founder of Mimi Castle, the last governor of Bessarabia, Constantin Mimi, with The Governor series of wines. Noble aromas and complex tastes waft through the bottles. The wine produced from the extraordinary 2018 vintage is considered by many to be one of the best in the region.

Restaurant

Fine dining restaurants and traditional bars

Passion and dedication have made the chefs at Castel Mimi perfectly blend the traditional with the modern. With care for people and nature, we select natural and delicious products so that we have minimal impact on the environment and offer high quality food on the plates served to each guest.

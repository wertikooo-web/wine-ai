National Wine Day 2025

October 4, 12:00 PM – 10:00 PMJoin us to celebrate the most important autumn event – Wine Festival – in the authentic atmosphere of the Carlevana Winery.Expect unforgettable experiences, selected fine wines, and music for the soul.

Day Program:• Festive welcome with a glass of Carlevana wine• Guided tour through the historic cellars• BBQ Zone & Open Kitchen (for an additional fee)• Folk dance masterclass with our ensemble• Special guest of the program: Geta Burlacu• Sandu Focșa Trio• Evening finale – DJ Party & dances around the bonfire• Gift for every guest: a bottle of memorable wine in honor of Wine Festival

Ticket Prices (without transport):• Early Bird – 400 MDL (until 15.09)• Standard – 450 MDL (until sold out)• Last Minute – 500 MDL (48 hours before the event)

Transport – 100 MDL round tripBus departure point: ONVV info point (Stefan cel Mare St., intersection with Banulescu Bodoni).

Children:• 7–12 years old (without transport) – 150 MDL• 7–12 years old (with transport) – 250 MDL• Up to 7 years old – free

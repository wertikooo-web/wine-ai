Selections mondiales des vins

Our wines continue to be appreciated internationally!

RUBINUS 2022 and RARA NEAGRA 2023 were awarded at Sélections Mondiales des Vins Canada, the competition with the largest international representation outside Europe.

The international jury recognized the quality and elegance of these wines at the 32nd edition, held between October 9 and 11, 2025, in Montreal, Canada.

We are proud to share this achievement and continue to bring the excellence of Moldovan wines to the international stage.

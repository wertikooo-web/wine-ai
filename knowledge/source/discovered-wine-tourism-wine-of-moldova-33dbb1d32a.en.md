Wine Tourism

It is the people that make wine tourism in Moldova special. A tourist to the Republic of Moldova will come across genuine, warm, and friendly people and wherever such meetings occur they also offer a visitor the opportunity to enjoy the traditional food of Moldova. Wine tourism experiences in Moldova include the famous underground wine cellars of “Cricova” and “Milestii Mici”, the latter being included in the Guinness Book of Records for the largest wine collection in the world. Tourists to Moldova can expect to uncover an incredible array of amazing wineries who’s welcome will include wine tasting tours, delicious local Moldovan food and wonderful stories counting the history of the estates.

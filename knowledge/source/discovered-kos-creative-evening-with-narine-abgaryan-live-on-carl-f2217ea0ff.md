Creative evening with Narine Abgaryan: “Live On”

We invite you to meet Narine Abgaryan, a writer whose words help us “live on.” It will be a conversation without elaborate decorations, but with real depth. A conversation about pain and light, about what makes us human. About compassion. About how important simple things are today: a meeting, a glance, warmth, a cup of tea, the opportunity to just be there.

Carlevana Winery is one of the partners of the event.

September 12 at the Chekhov Theater at 75 Vlaju Pyrkelab Street.

6:30 p.m. – wine tasting from CARLEVANA

7:00 p.m. – start of the creative evening

Tickets can be purchased here: https://livetickets.md/ru/event/334–seara-literara-cu-narine-abgaryan-dalshe-zhit

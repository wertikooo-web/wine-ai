The only winery in Moldova whose legacy has been carried through three generations, shaped by a unique terroir in the Protected Geographical Indication region of Ștefan Vodă.

A tradition built over time, internationally acclaimed wines, and a contemporary vision define the world of Sălcuța.

“Winemaking has been the path I’ve followed since childhood — an odyssey, the revelation of my purpose, and a lifelong journey devoted to crafting remarkable wines that embody the purest expression of local tradition, spirit, and dedication.”

EUGEN PÎSLARU, Winery Founder

About the Collection

The APERTUS wine series marks 30 years since the founding of Sălcuța Winery — a unique story shaped by three generations. It is an invitation to remain open to rediscovering the beauty of Moldovan wine. The wines in this collection stand out through complexity, balance, and elegance, reflecting the maturity and craftsmanship refined over time. Each glass of APERTUS reveals the depth of the terroir and the passion behind its creation, turning every tasting into an authentic and memorable experience.

About the Collection

A meticulously aged wine collection, matured in French oak barrels to reveal character and individuality. Named EPIZŌD, the collection reflects the core vision of the Sălcuța family: a wine is a long journey toward excellence, shaped through multiple stages by the winemaker. Each wine carries the imprint of the three winemakers and represents an episode within an ongoing work of art.

About the Collection

The ENO Collection redefines purity and the noble character of Sălcuța’s signature grape varieties. The ENO wines embody a living message of harmony between grapes selected from Sălcuța’s finest vineyards and the modernist vision of our winemakers.

About the Collection

Inspired by the tranquility and refinement of the vineyard landscape, the VALVERDE wine collection brings to the glass the freshness and balance of a gentle wave gliding gracefully across the Sălcuța vineyards. VALVERDE highlights the elegance of international grape varieties and the modern spirit of winemaking, turning every glass into a moment of genuine pleasure.

The musical performance “CHECK-UP” with Danila Kozlovsky – degustation

Carlevana Winery invites you to discover a unique experience, where the atmosphere of the event will combine with the flavors of our select wines—each with its own unique aroma and character.

The wine selection for the event includes: Raritet Rubinus, Raritet Alb de Mereni, Renaissance Muscat, Carlevana Apple Cider, and to add a surprise to the evening’s atmosphere, we will present a new brut sparkling wine, recently created by Carlevana — with a delicate effervescence and refreshing taste. Each glass will be a moment of pleasure that will delight the senses and delight those who try it.

We look forward to seeing you at the performance to enjoy special moments together, and Carlevana wines will be available for tasting and purchase in the foyer.

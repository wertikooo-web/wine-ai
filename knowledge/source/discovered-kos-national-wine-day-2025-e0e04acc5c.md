National Wine Day 2025

Carlevana Winery took part in International Wine Day 2025, which was held in Moldova on October 4-5.

We welcomed guests at our stand on the Great National Assembly Square. There were many interesting conversations, non-stop wine tastings, and merchandise sales in our improvised boutique. Despite the weather, many guests visited the stand and a record amount of wine was tasted.

At the same time, we welcomed guests at the winery. Over the two days of the festival, more than 300 guests visited the winery, and wine flowed freely alongside a wide variety of traditional snacks. Anyone who wished to do so could take part in a tour of the winery accompanied by the plant’s technologists themselves. The concert by Jet Burlacu was a huge success and ended with dancing until late in the evening.

We look forward to the next festival and to seeing you, dear lovers of Moldovan wine!
